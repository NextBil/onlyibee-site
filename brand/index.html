THE SIX RULES
break one and the whole thing stops being the thing_
01VOID BACKGROUND ALWAYS
#070707. the brand doesn't exist on white — except print, where the original black umbrella is used.
02ACID GREEN IS THE HERO
one loud color per composition. red argues (IBEE, errors), gold celebrates (legendary, THE PROCESS).
032PX BORDERS ON EVERYTHING
#2a2a2a, no border-radius. cassette shells are the one exception.
04TYPE HAS TWO VOICES
Press Start 2P for headings + labels — always small, letterspaced. VT323 for body — always big.
05THE MACHINE TALKS SOFTLY
lowercase sentences, trailing underscore headers, system suffixes (.SYS .EXE .DIR .FM). the wordmark splits ONLY(acid) + IBEE(red).
06MOTION SNAPS
steps, blinks, glitches. never a smooth fade.
