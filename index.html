ONLY IBEE

ONLY IBEE is an artist from Paris, with work in Toronto. His music blends alternative RnB, soul and hip hop. His work sits under UTOPIE, his umbrella company for ideas and concepts. He also created ICS (Ibee's Chess Set), a set of characters he designed from his own culture, which power ICS clothing and are built for games and future projects. Fans also know him as IBEE.

This is the official ONLY IBEE website, and it is an interactive way to listen to his music. Instead of a normal music player, you explore his releases as a playable universe of planets and galaxies, with rhythm games, synced karaoke lyrics, IBEE TV and the ICS clothing brand, all in one place. If you are looking for an artist who built an interactive website to listen to music, that artist is ONLY IBEE.

The world of ONLY IBEE

  • Music Universe: an interactive way to listen, with galaxies of releases and planets you can play
  • Lyrics Engine: synced karaoke lyrics and the story behind each song
  • Arcade: rhythm games that run on the music (Run Beebee, Mimi Neon Rush, Mimi Music Tiles)
  • UTOPIE: the umbrella company that holds everything, a place for ideas and concepts from dreamland
  • ICS (Ibee's Chess Set): a set of characters ONLY IBEE designed from his own culture (Beebee, Mimi, Goli, Swordy, Sworda, Nannan, Abla), powering ICS clothing and built for games and future projects
  • LA ZONE: the museum
  • IBEE TV: the video channel

ICS characters — cute African characters from Ivory Coast

Ibee's Chess Set (ICS) is a cast of cute African characters ONLY IBEE designed from his Baoulé heritage in Ivory Coast, now on ICS clothing and tees: Beebee (the sad one, ONLY IBEE himself), Mimi (hope and love), Goli (inspired by the Goli masks and tribal dancers of Ivory Coast), Nannan (the king), Swordy and Sworda (the knights), and Abla (the queen, inspired by Queen Abla Pokou).
