ABOUT ONLYIBEE

artist from Paris · work in Toronto

ONLY IBEE is an artist from Paris, with work in Toronto, and a creative brand. He was born in Ivory Coast and grew up in Paris.

His music blends alternative RnB, soul and hip hop. It all lives under UTOPIE, his umbrella company: a place for ideas and concepts where things that sound unreal get made real. Out of that come ICS, his chess-set characters, and a website that lets you listen to his music by exploring it rather than just pressing play. Fans also know him as IBEE.

THE MUSIC.

ONLY IBEE makes alternative RnB, soul and hip hop. His official website turns listening into something you move through: instead of a normal player, his releases are a playable universe of galaxies and planets, with synced karaoke lyrics and the story behind each song. If you were looking for an artist who built an interactive website to listen to music, that artist is ONLY IBEE.

Listen on Spotify, Apple Music, Deezer and YouTube.
